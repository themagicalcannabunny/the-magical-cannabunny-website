How to Use Magical Cannabunny Compost Tea

🌱 BEFORE SPRING PLANTING — WAKE UP YOUR SOIL

Spring is the beginning of a new growing cycle.

Before planting your vegetables, flowers, herbs, trees or other plants, apply MAGICAL CANNABUNNY COMPOST TEA to the soil and allow the biological community to become active before your new plants go into the ground.

Apply the tea evenly across the planting area and thoroughly moisten the soil. For gardens and planting beds, concentrate the application where future root systems will develop.

The goal is simple: establish biological activity before the new plants arrive.

For heavily disturbed, depleted or biologically inactive soil, repeated applications combined with finished compost, organic matter, mulch and minimal soil disturbance can help create a better environment for soil organisms.

🌿 DURING THE GROWING SEASON — FEED THE SOIL. SUPPORT THE ROOTS.

Use MAGICAL CANNABUNNY COMPOST TEA around established plants as a soil drench. Apply it directly to the soil around the plant’s root zone rather than simply pouring it onto the stem.

It can be used around:

  • Vegetable gardens
  • Flower gardens
  • Herbs
  • Trees
  • Shrubs
  • Raised beds
  • Lawns
  • Container gardens
  • Houseplants
  • Transplants
  • Established plants

Healthy soil is a biological system. Compost tea is intended to complement that system—not replace compost, mulch, good soil structure, proper watering, sunlight or responsible gardening practices.

🪴 HOUSEPLANTS & POTTED PLANTS — BRING THE LIVING SOIL INDOORS

Potted plants live in a very different environment from plants growing in the ground. A container has a limited amount of growing media, limited biological habitat and limited access to the larger soil ecosystem.

Apply MAGICAL CANNABUNNY COMPOST TEA as a soil drench, allowing it to move into the root zone. For houseplants, use a modest amount around the root zone rather than saturating the entire container. Allow the growing medium to drain normally.

A healthy microbial community around the roots can help support natural decomposition and nutrient cycling within the potting media.

Think of the pot as a miniature ecosystem.
The plant is not alone. Its roots interact with bacteria, fungi, organic matter, minerals, water and countless other biological processes.

🍂 AFTER HARVEST — DON’T LET THE CYCLE END WITH THE HARVEST.

When the growing season is over, the garden isn’t dead. It is preparing for the next cycle.

After harvesting your vegetables, flowers and other annual plants, apply MAGICAL CANNABUNNY COMPOST TEA to the remaining soil. This is an excellent time to return biological activity to the soil while incorporating appropriate organic matter, compost, plant residues or mulch.

🌎 Why Soil Microbiology Matters

Soil is not dirt. Soil is alive.

Bacteria, fungi, protozoa, earthworms and countless other organisms interact with organic matter, minerals, plant roots and one another. This biological community participates in the cycling of carbon and nutrients and helps create the environment in which plants grow.

Compost and compost tea can increase biological activity and help support organisms responsible for natural nutrient cycling. Compost itself contributes organic matter and habitat, while compost tea provides a liquid method of distributing microorganisms throughout the growing environment.

That is why we don’t look at compost tea simply as a fertilizer.

We look at it as biological infrastructure for the soil.

The objective isn’t to force nature to grow. The objective is to work with the biological systems that already make growth possible.

☀️ Application Tips

For best results:

  • Apply as a soil drench around the root zone.
  • Apply to moist soil rather than bone-dry soil.
  • Spring applications can help establish biological activity before planting.
  • Apply after transplanting to help introduce biology around the new root zone.
  • Continue applications during the growing season as part of an overall soil-management program.
  • Apply after harvest in the fall to help continue the biological cycle.
  • Combine with finished compost, organic matter and mulch for a more complete soil-building program.
  • Avoid applying immediately before harsh chemical treatments that could negatively affect microbial life.
  • Store and handle the tea according to the product’s specific instructions and use fresh biological products promptly when directed.

FOR EDIBLE CROPS

Because this product originates from an animal-manure-based biological system, follow the product’s specific application and harvest-interval instructions for edible crops. For food safety, do not assume that a compost tea is equivalent to potable water or a sterilized fertilizer.
