THE MAGICAL PART ISN’T THE POOP. IT’S THE LOOP.

HOLY SH!T represents the philosophy behind THE MAGICAL CANNABUNNY:

Food → Animal → Manure → Microbiology → Soil → Plant → Food → Life

This is the biological loop we call Regenerative Medicinal Farming.

The objective isn’t simply to grow more.

It is to regenerate the system that grows.

Healthy soil is alive with microorganisms, fungi, organic matter, roots and countless biological relationships. By returning organic materials to the soil instead of treating soil as an inert growing medium, we work with the ecological cycles that make agriculture possible in the first place.

FROM PEEP’S POOP TO THE PLATE

The ultimate purpose of regenerative farming isn’t the manure.

It’s the food.

We believe the future of agriculture should look at the entire biological system—from soil microbiology to plants, animals and humans—and ask how those systems can work together rather than against one another.

This is where our concept of ECOLOGICALLY MEDICINAL begins:

Living in a way that works with biological systems instead of constantly replacing them with industrial inputs.

The Magical Cannabunny research explores how plants, animals, fungi, microorganisms and the endocannabinoid system/endocannabinoidome may fit into a larger ecological understanding of health and biological relationships.

HOLY SH!T is one small—and very important—piece of that bigger picture.

Because before you can talk about medicine in the plant…
you have to talk about the soil that grew the plant.
